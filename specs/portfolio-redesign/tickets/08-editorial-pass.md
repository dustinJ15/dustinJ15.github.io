# 08: Editorial pass, every page

**What to build:** A reader gets writing that sounds like Dustin on every page. The full site
gets an editorial pass: the home page opening states what he does in a line, the case studies
keep arguing rather than listing, and the connective tissue that was written to fill a layout
gets cut.

Every em-dash goes. He reads them as an AI tell, and they are currently everywhere. Replacing
one is not mechanical: the sentence gets rewritten so the punctuation is not missed.

Alt text and meta descriptions are published text and get the same treatment, including the
em-dash rule.

Two things the pass must not do: reintroduce the retired spreadsheet motif in any form, and
frame any of it as a career change. It is a return, and no page explains the gap.

When this lands, the gate's em-dash check stops being advisory and starts failing the build.
